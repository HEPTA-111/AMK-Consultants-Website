Who we are

Strategic Insight. Meaningful Partnerships. Long-Term Value.

At AMK Consultants, we believe successful investment begins with a clear understanding of opportunity, strong commercial relationships and well-structured transactions.

Operating as Anchor Marque Kapital Consultants LLP, our approach combines strategic advisory with a focus on connecting project sponsors, financiers and institutional stakeholders to support investment and business development across key sectors.
